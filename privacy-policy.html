Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Vibecop SaaS & AI Startup Solutions collects, uses, and protects information when you access our platform. We value your privacy and aim to provide a secure, seamless experience while exploring our AI solutions, SaaS tools, and business services.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information when you create an account, subscribe to newsletters, request demos, contact support, or purchase SaaS products. This may include your name, email address, phone number, company details, and billing information.

We also collect non-personal information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and general platform usage data. This helps us enhance platform performance, optimize AI features, and improve your overall user experience.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Process subscriptions, orders, and service requests efficiently.
  • Respond to inquiries and provide personalized support for our AI tools and SaaS solutions.
  • Send important updates, confirmations, or promotional offers (if opted in).

Non-personal data helps us analyze platform usage, maintain security, prevent fraudulent activity, and ensure smooth operations. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

  • Information is used to manage accounts, subscriptions, and service requests.
  • Platform usage data helps improve performance, navigation, and user experience.
  • Security measures are in place to protect user data and maintain safe operations.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted third-party providers such as payment processors, cloud services, or technical support providers when necessary to operate our platform and deliver services.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law, to protect our legal rights, or to ensure the security of our SaaS environment.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our platform may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve functionality, analyze performance, and personalize your experience. Cookies help remember preferences, feature usage, and platform settings. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our platform, services, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@opzio.com
